
# 🎈 Remove Balloons

**Remove Balloons** is a simple and challenging arcade game where players must pop balloons as they appear on the screen and aim for the highest possible score.

## 🎮 About the Game

The objective is to pop balloons before they accumulate on the screen. As more balloons appear, the challenge increases. If too many balloons remain on the screen, the game ends.

Test your speed, reaction time, and accuracy to achieve the highest score!

## 🕹️ How to Play

- Pop balloons as they appear.
- Try to prevent balloons from accumulating.
- Keep the screen clear to avoid losing.
- Earn the highest score possible.

## 🎯 Objective

Survive as long as possible, pop as many balloons as you can, and beat your high score.
